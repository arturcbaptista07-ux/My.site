Who am I?

I am 19 years old and I come from Portugal, from a small town near Lisbon called Ericeira. Ericeira is well known for being a World Surfing Reserve and for its strong connection to the ocean. I personally enjoy surfing, so growing up there made the sea and surf an important part of my life.

Why did I choose VIA?

I chose VIA University College because it seemed like a good option for me and because I was very interested in this specific programme. I also liked the fact that it is located in Horsens and that VIA has a practical approach to learning with projects and teamwork.

Why Software Technology Engineering?

I chose Software Technology Engineering because I think it is a very interesting field with a lot of potential for the future. It is also an area that connects well with what I would like to do professionally, and I believe there are still many opportunities for growth and innovation in technology.
